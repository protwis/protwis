Peptide hormone analog bioactivities

A literature-annotated dataset of bioactivities, mostly potency and Emax values, for physiological peptide hormones and their analogs. Each entry was annotated with a standardized spreadsheet and imported into GPCRdb.

Contents

  • Class A (6,626 rows): neurokinin, vasopressin, orexin-A, substance P, MSH, GnRH, OT, ACTH, angiotensin II, Ccl1-28, Cx3Cl1, Cxcl1-17 and Xcl1.
  • Class B1 (3,012 rows): α/β-CGRP, adrenomedullin (ADM), amylin, calcitonin (CT), gastric inhibitory peptide (GIP), GNRH, glucagon, PACAP, PTH, PTHRP, secretin, TIP39, urocortin-1 and VIP.

Use it to

  • See which analogs have already been tested, and which have not.
  • Analyse structure-activity relationships across published studies.

The standardized annotation spreadsheet is available upon request.
